# Project-Ideas: a catalog of ideas for games, businesses, and nonprofit projects

_Concepts for games, mechanics, business opportunities, and nonprofit initiatives collected over the years and made available for reference, inspiration, and collaboration._

**Summary**: I brought together in Project-Ideas a collection of ideas for games, businesses, and social initiatives that I have developed over the years. Rather than presenting finished projects, I want to share starting points that can inspire new solutions, take shape through the work of others, or evolve through collaboration. I believe a good idea should not be limited by its creator's ability to execute it. For this reason, I made the catalog publicly available, allowing anyone to explore, adapt, and develop these proposals. In this article, I present twenty game concepts that represent part of this collection and an invitation to turn ideas into projects.

---

In 2024, I began organizing a collection of ideas I had accumulated over the years, initially focused on game development. My interest in game development led me to explore concepts, mechanics, and narrative possibilities, as well as experiment with tools such as MonoGame and Godot. Some proposals reached the prototyping stage, but none progressed far enough to become a complete product.

The main difficulty in moving these proposals forward was not a lack of willingness, but rather the combination of available time and the skills required. Balancing professional responsibilities with side projects naturally imposes limitations on the time one can dedicate to them. Furthermore, although my experience in software development and solution architecture provides a solid technical foundation, it does not replace hands-on experience in specific areas of game production. Game design, narrative development, user experience, art direction, and animation involve specialized knowledge that requires practice and expertise. Developing a game is a multidisciplinary effort, and recognizing this distinction is important when assessing what it takes to turn an idea into reality.

Over time, the collection expanded beyond games to include business opportunities and nonprofit initiatives. This expansion reflects a broader interest in project conception, problem identification, and exploring different ways of turning them into concrete initiatives. The proposals have varying levels of maturity: some are initial concepts, while others feature more elaborate mechanics, systems, structures, or narrative elements. This is not a portfolio of validated projects or documents ready for implementation, but rather a collection of starting points that can be explored and developed further.

The decision to publish this material on GitHub came from a simple realization: the potential of these ideas should not be limited by my individual ability to execute them. A proposal that its creator cannot find the means to develop may still spark the interest of someone with different skills, resources, or perspectives. Sharing allows these ideas to serve as inspiration, be incorporated into other people's projects, or take on new directions through collaboration.

This initiative is also connected to how I approach project conception and structuring. An idea, by itself, does not necessarily represent a viable opportunity. Its evolution depends on factors such as the problem to be solved or experience to be delivered, the target audience, the value proposition, technical feasibility, and execution capabilities. Not all proposals in the catalog have undergone this type of assessment, and there is no intention of presenting them as opportunities ready for commercial exploitation. The goal is to make material available that can serve as a foundation for new possibilities, leaving it to each interested party to determine what makes sense to develop and under what conditions.

Although the catalog covers different categories, this article is dedicated to games. I selected twenty concepts that I find particularly interesting, organized according to my personal preferences, without claiming to establish an objective ranking of quality, originality, or commercial potential. Some proposals stand out for their mechanics, others for their narrative or fictional universe. The selection reflects my influences and preferences, and the intention is to present the reasoning behind each concept, allowing readers to discover proposals they might not otherwise have considered.

I am also interested in collaborating with developers and designers who would like to further develop any of these proposals. My contribution would primarily involve conception, structuring, and solution architecture, complementing skills in game design, art, narrative, and game development. The goal does not necessarily have to be a commercial product: exploring a mechanic, testing a hypothesis, or refining a concept are also worthwhile outcomes.

The complete catalog is available on GitHub in the [Project-Ideas](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/) repository, under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license, which allows the content to be shared, adapted, and used commercially, provided the original authorship is credited. There is no obligation to request permission or offer compensation to use the proposals. Nevertheless, if any of them results in a commercially successful implementation, a voluntary contribution in recognition of the original concept would always be welcome. This is a possibility, not a condition for using the material.

Below, I present the twenty selected game concepts:

### 20. [Just Breathe: Restarting One Step at a Time](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/just-breathe-restarting-one-step-at-a-time/README.pt-BR.md)

This is a narrative adventure about personal recovery, starring a young hikikomori who lives isolated in his room, withdrawn from society and dependent on his family. After realizing the impact of his behavior on those around him, he decides to try to change his life. The journey does not begin with major goals, but with seemingly mundane tasks, such as organizing his room, taking care of his personal hygiene, and establishing a routine. Gradually, these small achievements pave the way for greater challenges, such as regaining responsibilities, overcoming the fear of leaving home, and rebuilding social connections.

The proposal's distinguishing feature lies in how it approaches character development. Instead of traditional progression based on levels, equipment, or combat skills, advancement comes through the gradual recovery of autonomy and self-esteem. The process is also nonlinear: setbacks, insecurities, and frustrations can undermine previous achievements, requiring the player to find the strength to start over. It is a concept that seeks to turn everyday aspects of personal recovery into game mechanics, exploring a theme rarely addressed in the narrative adventure genre.

### 19. [Project Hope: The Seed of Change](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/project-hope-the-seed-of-change/README.pt-BR.md)

It starts with a relatively simple premise: a young man wins the lottery and decides to use his fortune to transform the lives of orphans and homeless people. Rather than simply distributing money, he establishes a support network that starts modestly and can grow to include housing, educational institutions, healthcare services, and vocational training centers. The player takes responsibility for managing this initiative, balancing financial resources, infrastructure, and the needs of a population with very different backgrounds and circumstances.

The proposal combines simulation and management with character development and social storytelling. Each person taken in has their own talents, difficulties, experiences, and goals, requiring solutions that take individual needs into account. Some first need to regain stability, while others are already in a position to study, work, or contribute to the community. The complexity lies precisely in understanding that providing resources does not, by itself, solve problems. In addition to financial management, the player must deal with bureaucracy, prejudice, internal conflicts, and unexpected crises, while following the effects of their decisions over time. The intended outcome goes beyond building an efficient organization: it is about creating the conditions for people to regain their autonomy and eventually help others.

### 18. [Vessel Conflict: The Masquerade of Centuries](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/vessel-conflict-the-masquerade-of-centuries/README.pt-BR.md)

It presents a centuries-old rivalry between two brothers who possess the extraordinary ability to transfer their consciousness into other human bodies, completely assuming their hosts' identities. Initially, both share this ability, but they follow opposing paths. While the older brother uses this power to infiltrate governments, institutions, and organizations, quietly building a global influence network, the younger one tries to stop his plans. Over time, however, he realizes that upholding his moral principles may not be enough to confront an adversary who has no such constraints. The rivalry evolves into a conflict between two forces that become increasingly similar in their methods.

The consciousness transfer mechanic is the central element of the experience and opens up interesting possibilities for action, strategy, espionage, and psychological suspense. Each host offers different characteristics, abilities, and social positions, allowing the player to infiltrate organizations, manipulate events, and move through different historical periods. The transfer itself occurs at the moment of death, eliminating the original host and allowing the brothers to persist across centuries. More than a conventional hero-versus-villain conflict, the concept explores the limits of identity and morality: when someone can assume different bodies and lives, what truly defines their existence? And to what extent can one fight an enemy without eventually adopting their methods?

### 17. [Play The Symphony: An Orchestral Experience](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/play-the-symphony-an-orchestral-experience/README.pt-BR.md)

It proposes a musical experience in which the player stops being merely a spectator or solo performer and takes on the role of an orchestra member. Inspired by Sound! Euphonium (Hibike! Euphonium), the game allows players to choose from different instruments in the string, woodwind, brass, and percussion families, each with its own sheet music, techniques, and challenges. Performances require players to follow the conductor, keep the tempo, control dynamics, and execute entrances at the right moment, maintaining synchronization with the other musicians.

The proposal also includes a career mode in which the player progresses musically through rehearsals, auditions, performances, and competitions, earning opportunities to join more prestigious ensembles. The most interesting aspect, however, lies in the collective evaluation: performance does not depend exclusively on individual accuracy, but on the quality of the orchestra's execution as a whole. A technically competent musician who cannot keep up with the ensemble can compromise the performance. The possibility of online cooperation, with different players taking on different instruments, expands on this idea and turns the game into a collaborative experience. The goal is not to be the star of the performance, but to contribute to making it happen in the best possible way.

### 16. [Family Matters: Wealth & Governance](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/family-matters-wealth-governance/README.pt-BR.md)

It brings the power dynamics, intrigue, and succession mechanics of games like Crusader Kings into the contemporary world of major business dynasties. The player takes on the role of chairman of a powerful family, responsible for preserving and expanding a fortune built over generations. Managing companies, investments, and properties is part of the job, but the main challenge lies within the family structure itself. Each member has ambitions, interests, alliances, and resentments that influence decisions and can jeopardize the continuity of the empire. Disputes over positions, strategic marriages, conflicts between heirs, and attempts to seize control of the businesses are all part of this environment.

The experience combines business management and family governance with political intrigue and personal relationships. The player must delegate responsibilities, prepare successors, manage conflicts, and negotiate with executives, investors, politicians, and other influential families. Seemingly administrative decisions can trigger internal disputes, while personal alliances can become important tools for strengthening the dynasty's position. Generational progression adds another strategic layer: preserving the fortune is not just about ensuring financial results in the present, but also preparing a structure capable of surviving the succession itself. It is a proposal that explores the relationship between economic power, family legacy, and individual interests, showing that managing a large organization can be easier than managing the people who control it.

### 15. [The Debt Collector: Dead Assets Pay No Debts](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/the-debt-collector-dead-assets-pay-no-debts/README.pt-BR.md)

It takes debt collection into an intergalactic universe, where the player works for the Intergalactic Bank, recovering loans across planets, space stations, and decaying colonies. Debtors range from indebted aliens and dangerous criminals to corporations and mafia organizations. To perform his duties, the protagonist holds a special license that allows him to resort to intimidation, negotiation, physical combat, and other coercive methods. There is, however, one non-negotiable rule: the debtor must not die. If that happens, the entire debt is transferred to the collector himself, turning a credit recovery operation into a personal financial problem.

This restriction creates an interesting dynamic between action, negotiation, and risk management. Each collection requires assessing the debtor's profile, the environment, and the possible consequences before deciding how to approach the situation. Some will agree to negotiate, while others will try to flee, hire mercenaries, or set up ambushes. As the player advances in his career, he receives more valuable contracts and faces increasingly dangerous situations, while discovering that certain debts conceal much larger connections to organized crime, political interests, and interplanetary conspiracies. The concept combines space exploration and combat with biting humor about the logic of a financial institution that considers virtually any delinquency solvable through an in-person visit.

### 14. [Mindjack: Borrowed Lives](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/mindjack-borrowed-lives/README.pt-BR.md)

Mindjack is a psychological thriller and narrative survival game in which the player works for a clandestine service that provides assistance in extreme situations. Through encrypted calls, the player remotely takes control of the minds of people involved in kidnappings, pursuits, escapes, and other dangerous circumstances, guiding them to safety. Each operation features a different character, with their own physical and psychological limitations. These are not specially trained operatives, but ordinary people who may be injured, frightened, or simply unprepared to face dangerous situations.

The main distinguishing feature lies in the relationship between the player and their clients. The service establishes one absolute rule: never question what the person has done or the circumstances that led them into that situation. However, as operations progress, contradictions and incomplete information begin to reveal that not everyone is necessarily innocent, just as their pursuers may have unexpected motivations. Survival depends on planning, improvisation, and environmental exploration, but the real conflict becomes moral. After all, does saving someone necessarily mean doing the right thing? The proposal combines tactical challenges with a narrative that calls into question the legitimacy of the player's own actions.

### 13. [Drone Saga: Undercover Ace](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/drone-saga-undercover-ace/README.pt-BR.md)

Drone Saga follows a drone enthusiast who starts out participating in local competitions for fun but eventually attracts the attention of police and military organizations due to his exceptional piloting skills. Recognition in tournaments opens the door to an unexpected career involving secret infiltration, reconnaissance, and intelligence-gathering missions. The protagonist begins leading a double life: publicly, he is a professional pilot who competes in tournaments, secures sponsorships, and builds his reputation; behind the scenes, he carries out increasingly complex and dangerous clandestine operations.

The proposal combines drone piloting, career progression, and espionage, exploring two activities that share technical skills but have very different objectives. The player must improve their skills, modify equipment, and develop piloting techniques to meet both the demands of competitions and the needs of secret operations. Reputation management also becomes important, since public success must not reveal the existence of clandestine activities. The contrast between a sporting career and espionage work offers an interesting structure for alternating precision and speed challenges with missions that require discretion and planning.

### 12. [Around the World in 8 Days: The Influencer Challenge](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/around-the-world-in-8-days-the-influencer-challenge/README.pt-BR.md)

Around the World in 8 Days proposes a strategy and time-management adventure in which an influencer accepts an unusual bet: to travel around the world in just eight days to prove she is the greatest content creator on the planet. The journey involves visiting different countries and continents, publishing content in real time, and taking part in local challenges capable of attracting new followers. Each destination represents an opportunity to expand reach and engagement, but also requires logistical decisions about routes, transportation, and available time. The competition takes the form of a global reality show in which the protagonist must remain relevant while racing against the clock.

The distinguishing feature lies in turning social media dynamics into a strategic management system. It is not enough to reach destinations quickly: relevant content must be produced, unexpected events must be leveraged, and one's public image must be managed. Rivals may sabotage recordings, fans may interfere with travel plans, and a post can either go viral or trigger a reputation crisis. The player must balance speed, creativity, planning, and engagement, considering that every decision affects their performance in the competition. The proposal uses the world of digital influencers as the backdrop for a contest in which the most unpredictable opponent may not be another contestant, but the algorithm itself.

### 11. [Hamster vs. Capybara: Whisker Warfare](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/hamster-vs-capybara-whisker-warfare/README.pt-BR.md)

Hamster vs. Capybara depicts a completely absurd military war between two factions of small animals fighting for territorial dominance. On one side are the hamsters, agile and resourceful, specializing in improvised constructions and creative equipment. On the other are the capybaras, resilient and organized, favoring collective strength, defense, and territorial control. The conflict takes place in environments inspired by everyday human life, transformed into miniature battlefields where ordinary objects take on military functions. Hamster wheels can become tanks, toys turn into aircraft, and food is converted into ammunition and supplies.

Despite its humorous premise, the structure is that of a military strategy game featuring resource management, base building, and troop organization. The two factions have their own characteristics and units, encouraging different approaches to conquering territories and fighting the enemy. In addition to recruiting soldiers and developing equipment, the player must manage logistics, morale, and offensive and defensive operations. The interactive environment and unpredictable events add chaotic situations to the battles, while the asymmetry between the factions allows for different strategies. The fun lies precisely in the contrast between the tiny scale of the combatants and the scale of the military operations they command.

### 10. [Rocket & Olive: Fast and Furries](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/rocket-olive-fast-and-furries/README.pt-BR.md)

Rocket & Olive is a platform and action game starring two anthropomorphic characters: Rocket, a raccoon, and Olive, an otter. Together, they must stop the plans of a mad scientist who threatens to put the world in danger. The adventure unfolds across levels filled with obstacles, traps, moving platforms, and enemies working to carry out the villain's plans. Each character has their own characteristics and abilities, allowing players to explore different ways of navigating environments and overcoming challenges. Gadgets and inventions complement these abilities, providing tools to overcome obstacles and reach seemingly inaccessible areas.

The proposal draws inspiration from games such as Donkey Kong Country, Ratchet & Clank, and It Takes Two, combining traditional platforming, combat, exploration, and puzzle-solving. Switching between Rocket and Olive is one of the central elements of the experience, since their complementary abilities allow players to approach situations in different ways. The player must take advantage of these differences to progress through the levels, fight the scientist's minions, and overcome environmental challenges. The lighthearted tone, creative gadgets, and absurd situations give the adventure its personality, while progression through themed environments and boss battles provide a familiar genre structure.

### 9. [Star Piracy: Return of the Scoundrel](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/star-piracy-return-of-the-scoundrel/README.pt-BR.md)

Star Piracy is a space exploration adventure with RPG and management elements, starring a Golden Age pirate who ends up frozen during an expedition in Antarctica and awakens centuries later in a futuristic civilization where humanity has already conquered space. Upon discovering that the world has become too safe, automated, and monotonous for his adventurous spirit, he decides to take his old profession beyond Earth's atmosphere. Arrogant, hard-drinking, and highly flexible in his morality, the protagonist is not particularly interested in adapting to the new society. His goal is to explore the unknown, accumulate wealth, and build a reputation as the greatest intergalactic pirate of all time.

The experience combines planetary exploration, space combat, trade, smuggling, and crew management. The player can recruit companions, customize their ship, discover lost civilizations, and search for treasures and legendary relics. Relationships with different factions and decisions made during expeditions also influence the protagonist's reputation and opportunities throughout the universe. The proposal takes inspiration from classic pirate adventures such as Sid Meier's Pirates! and transports them into a science fiction setting, preserving the spirit of freedom, discovery, and unpredictability characteristic of this type of journey.

### 8. [Shabby Casino: The House Always Loses](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/shabby-casino-the-house-always-loses/README.pt-BR.md)

Shabby Casino is a business simulation and management game in which the player inherits a rundown casino in Las Vegas from their grandparents. The protagonist is an eccentric fellow, passionate about gambling and known for making questionable financial decisions. The inheritance, however, is far from a fortune: the establishment is practically bankrupt, with deteriorating facilities, few customers, and a forgotten reputation. The mission is to rebuild the business and turn it into one of the city's leading entertainment destinations, facing competition and the challenges of managing an operation that starts practically from scratch.

The main source of humor is the fact that the owner is also a compulsive gambler. While trying to control the casino's finances, he participates in the betting himself, creating situations in which his personal interests directly conflict with the business's financial health. In addition, facilities must be renovated, employees hired, different customer profiles attracted, gaming tables managed, and professional gamblers capable of compromising the house's profitability dealt with. Inspectors, eccentric employees, and unpredictable events complete the setting. The proposal combines business management and economic simulation with humor, exploring the contrast between the ambition to build a major enterprise and the owner's inability to keep his own finances in order.

### 7. [Wizard’s Debt: Student Loan Hell](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/wizards-debt-student-loan-hell/README.pt-BR.md)

Wizard's Debt is a fantasy RPG with simulation and management elements in which the protagonist is a magic student who discovers too late the abusive terms of the student loan he took out from the goblin bank. To prevent the institution from enforcing a contractual clause allowing it to seize his soul as collateral, he must find ways to generate income and pay off the debt before accumulated interest makes the situation irreversible. His job opportunities are far from conventional: they range from cleaning sewers using light spells to delivering pizzas via teleportation and providing services to supernatural creatures.

The proposal combines dungeon exploration, combat, magical skill development, and financial management. The player must choose between relatively safe but poorly paid jobs and dangerous expeditions in search of treasures and artifacts capable of accelerating debt repayment. The problem is that equipment, potions, repairs, and unexpected expenses also consume resources and can increase indebtedness. The goblin bank monitors the protagonist's financial progress, while interest continues to accumulate regardless of his efforts. The satirical humor comes from transposing familiar problems, such as student loans, abusive contracts, and difficulties entering the job market, into a fantasy universe where the consequence of defaulting is literally losing one's soul.

### 6. [Transient Realms: The Impersistent World](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/transient-realms-the-impersistent-world/README.pt-BR.md)

Transient Realms is a fantasy RPG with drama and procedural exploration elements, set in a world where nothing stays the same. Cities change their layouts, places disappear, people assume different identities, and events are replaced as if they had never existed. The protagonist is the only person capable of perceiving these changes, although his own memories are often vague and fragmented. He has lived with these inconsistencies since childhood, until one day he returns home and discovers that his family and every reference point in his life have been erased from reality. While the other inhabitants accept the current world as if it had always been that way, he retains fragments of memories from previous versions.

The journey involves exploring constantly changing territories, investigating the origin of this phenomenon, and trying to understand why the protagonist is the only being capable of recognizing these changes. Procedural generation is not limited to map creation: characters, cultures, cities, and relationships can also change between different versions of the world. This creates a distinctive narrative dynamic in which friendships, family bonds, and achievements are subject to the same impermanence that affects the environment. The most interesting aspect of the proposal lies in the tension between building a life and knowing that everything can disappear at any moment. More than discovering the rules of an unstable universe, the player must find meaning in experiences whose permanence cannot be guaranteed.

### 5. [Ghost Office: Post-Mortem Management](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/ghost-office-post-mortem-management/README.pt-BR.md)

Ghost Office is a business simulation and management game with supernatural humor. Following the death of its chairman, a company collapses as a result of years of excessively centralized management, in which the former owner controlled practically every decision and never prepared his heirs to take over the business. Unable to accept that the organization he built over his lifetime might disappear, his ghost remains in the office and decides to continue running the company from beyond the grave. Since he cannot interact directly with the world of the living, he finds an unconventional solution: possessing employees to carry out tasks, increase productivity, and solve operational problems. In more delicate situations, he can also take control of customers to influence negotiations and close deals.

The possession mechanic is the proposal's main distinguishing feature and functions as a management tool, allowing the player to compensate for employees' limitations and the heirs' lack of preparation. The player must manage departments, projects, meetings, financial resources, and corporate crises, deciding when to use supernatural abilities to interfere with operations. However, the former chairman remains the same controlling boss he was before, convinced that no one can do anything properly without his intervention. The humor comes precisely from this contradiction: even after death, he cannot abandon the habits that contributed to the company's problems. The proposal combines business management and supernatural elements to satirically explore topics such as centralized decision-making, succession, leadership dependency, and organizational continuity.

### 4. [Anhangabaú: The Devil's River](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/anhangaba%C3%BA-the-devils-river/README.pt-BR.md)

Anhangabaú is an action-adventure game with dark fantasy and supernatural horror elements, set deep beneath the Anhangabaú Valley in São Paulo. Beneath the city's concrete lies an ancient river, imprisoned for generations by a seal that prevents its influence from reaching the surface. Its waters, however, are not natural: they are a black, supernatural substance capable of materializing nightmares, distorting environments, and transforming creatures into monsters. When the seal breaks, this force begins invading tunnels, underground passages, and foundations, progressively distorting urban reality. The player takes on the role of a guardian belonging to a forgotten lineage, whose mission is to investigate the breach and prevent the entity from reaching the surface.

The experience combines exploration of interconnected underground environments, combat against supernatural creatures, mystery investigation, and skill progression. The main challenge lies in the Colossi of the Devil's River, large-scale ancient entities, each with their own characteristics, attack patterns, and vulnerabilities. The influence of the black substance also transforms the environments as exploration progresses, causing initially recognizable spaces to become increasingly hostile and unrecognizable. The proposal combines influences such as Dark Souls, Bloodborne, and Shadow of the Colossus, bringing dark fantasy elements into a distinctly Brazilian urban setting. The choice of Anhangabaú is not merely aesthetic: it is a way of exploring the imagery of one of São Paulo's best-known areas from a supernatural perspective.

### 3. [The Anti-Mage: Justice in the Shadows](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/the-anti-mage-justice-in-the-shadows/README.pt-BR.md)

The Anti-Mage is an action RPG combining dark fantasy and narrative investigation, set in Universum Arcanum, a fantasy universe I have been developing through different concepts and stories. The narrative follows Alden, a young man of humble origins who grows up in a society where mages hold political, economic, and institutional power, while most of the population lives under unequal conditions. His late awakening to magic offers an opportunity to change his social standing, but does not eliminate the barriers imposed by his origins. During his journey, he makes a pact with a mysterious creature and acquires the ability to wield anti-magic, an exceptional power that allows him to disrupt magical traces, neutralize protections, and perform actions beyond the reach of conventional investigative methods.

With this ability, Alden begins operating on two fronts: during the day, he uses his institutional position to investigate crimes and fight corrupt organizations; in the shadows, he employs anti-magic to infiltrate protected locations, obtain information, and act against individuals who manage to evade conventional justice. The player follows his evolution from humble beginnings to his rise to the position of archmage, facing political conflicts, investigations, faction disputes, and decisions with long-term consequences. The central aspect of the narrative, however, lies in the protagonist's moral transformation: the more power he gains, the more he begins to believe that he alone can decide when the rules should be followed. The story already has a partially developed narrative structure, available in [The Anti-Mage: The Story](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/the-anti-mage-justice-in-the-shadows/story-pt-BR.md), which explores Alden's journey and the political and institutional conflicts of Veridion in greater depth.

### 2. [The Saga of Eldrin: The Cursed Crown](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/the-saga-of-eldrin-the-cursed-crown/README.pt-BR.md)

The Saga of Eldrin is a medieval fantasy narrative RPG set in Velmoria, an ancient empire fragmented into seven rival kingdoms following the death of its last emperor. The story follows Eldrin, an orphan who begins his journey as a thief on the streets of Ravencourt, until he is taken in by Garrick Thorne, a powerful merchant who introduces him to the world of trade, diplomacy, and struggles for influence. Through intelligence and ambition, Eldrin builds his own fortune, acquires properties, and gradually rises into the aristocracy. His journey takes him from merchant to baron, count, military commander, and duke, participating in the reunification of the former imperial territories. However, his rise awakens the hostility of the traditional nobility, who begin to see him as a threat to the established order.

After being betrayed by his allies and losing his titles, properties, and influence, Eldrin discovers evidence that he may be the last descendant of the imperial bloodline. From that point on, his quest to reclaim power takes on a personal dimension, marked by conspiracies, wars, losses, and doubts about his own origins. Unlike traditional narratives about ascending to the throne, the story does not end with the acquisition of the crown: the protagonist becomes progressively paranoid and ruthless, until he realizes that he is perpetuating the very cycle of conflict that destroyed the empire. The complete narrative has already been written and is available in [The Saga of Eldrin: The Story](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/the-saga-of-eldrin-the-cursed-crown/story.pt-BR.md). The concept combines narrative RPG, property management, trade, diplomacy, dynastic intrigue, and military strategy, drawing inspiration from Crusader Kings, Mount & Blade, and The Witcher. It is a proposal in which character progression is not limited to skill development or territorial conquest, but follows the transformation of someone who spends their life trying to seize power and ultimately destroys what they so desperately wanted to rule.

### 1. [The Lord of the Glitches: Bug of the Year Edition](https://github.com/cpzjunior/project-ideas/blob/main/game-concepts/advanced/the-lord-of-the-glitches-bug-of-the-year-edition/README.pt-BR.md)

The Lord of the Glitches is an adventure, action, and comedy game that explores the relationship between video games, bugs, and glitches. The idea came to me when I watched a video showing mammoths flying in Skyrim, one of the game's best-known bugs. I was never able to reproduce that specific glitch, but since I had already encountered and reproduced several others in different games, I began imagining what an experience built entirely around these anomalies would look like. Instead of treating programming errors as problems to be fixed, the proposal starts from the premise that they can be central elements of the fun and of the progression itself. In the story, an ordinary gamer is transported to a video game multiverse and encounters MissingNo, the legendary glitch creature from Pokémon Red & Blue, who becomes his guide. Together, they must investigate a wave of bugs and anomalies threatening different gaming universes.

The adventure travels through parody versions of well-known franchises, exploring their historical bugs, exploits, and unexpected behaviors. In a version of Skyrim, mammoths fly and dragons defy the laws of physics; in an Assassin's Creed parody, characters pass through invisible walls and ships behave absurdly; while in a reimagining of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, classic glitches such as clipping, bomb hovering, and wrong warps become tools for overcoming challenges. Progression depends precisely on discovering and using these glitches, allowing players to manipulate physics, move through environments, and bypass obstacles in unconventional ways. Behind the humor and references to gaming culture, there is also a satire of game standardization and excessive monetization, represented by a corporation seeking to control the multiverse. This concept takes first place not only because of its gameplay possibilities, but also because of its personal significance: it was the first game idea I ever wrote down and the one I feel most attached to. What began with a video of flying mammoths eventually became a proposal that explores precisely what makes video games unpredictable and, often, memorable.
