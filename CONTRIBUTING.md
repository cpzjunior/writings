# Contributing

This repository is a collection of my personal writings about technology, software, architecture, AI, management, and related topics.

The writings are authored and maintained by me. This repository does not accept articles or writings from other authors.

## What you can contribute

Contributions are welcome when they improve existing writings.

You can contribute with corrections to factual errors, typos, broken links, clarity, accuracy, references, examples, or relevant perspectives.

You can also suggest topics or questions worth exploring.

## Pull Requests

For changes to an existing writing, briefly explain what you changed and why.

For small corrections, no extensive explanation is necessary.

## Writing Style

Writings should favor clear and precise reasoning, distinction between facts, assumptions, and opinions, relevant examples and references, and consideration of meaningful counterarguments.

Disagreement is welcome. Contributions do not need to agree with my conclusions, but should provide a substantive perspective or help improve the writing.

## Editorial Decisions

All final editorial decisions remain with the maintainer. Contributions may be accepted, modified, or declined based on the scope and editorial direction of the repository.
